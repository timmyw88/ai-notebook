An **embedding** is a list of numbers that represents the *meaning* of a piece of text. Texts with similar meanings get similar numbers, so you can compare them with simple math.

Typical uses:

- Semantic search ("find notes about vector databases")
- Grouping similar documents
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
